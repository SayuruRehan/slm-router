# Paper Notes — Factual Confidence of LLMs: On Reliability and Robustness of Current Estimators

**Authors:** Mahaut et al.  
**Year / Venue:** 2024 / ACL  
**Peer-reviewed:** Yes  
**Verification:** https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-long.250/

## Problem
Assess reliability and robustness of factual-confidence estimators for LLMs.

## Setting / Data
Factual QA/confidence estimation under perturbations.

## Method
Benchmarks probability- and representation-based confidence estimators.

## Evaluation
Confidence discrimination/calibration and robustness.

## Key Finding
Confidence estimators vary in reliability and can be unstable under meaning-preserving changes.

## Limitations / Conflicting Evidence
Confidence is not a direct downstream-action residual-risk estimate.

## Relevance to TRACER
Supports combining multiple uncertainty signals and evaluating calibration carefully.

## Gap Evidence
ACRE predicts action-specific failure rather than treating confidence as a generic correctness proxy.
